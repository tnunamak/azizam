Run a gateway

Invite people

On this page
  • Before you invite anyone
  • Send an invitation
  • What the person does

Each person you invite gets their own account, their own app and their own key. Their access plan sets what they can use.

Before you invite anyone #

  • Choose an access plan. The plan decides which models and workflows a person can use. Read Access plans.
  • Make the gateway reachable. A person on another device cannot use 127.0.0.1: that address points to their own device. Set up an HTTPS address and secure cookies first. See Open the gateway to other devices.

Send an invitation #

  1. Open People, in the Gateway group of the menu.
  2. Create an invite.
  3. Choose the access plan and when the invite expires. The default is 7 days; the range is one minute to 30 days.
  4. Copy the invitation link, and send it to the person yourself.

The gateway does not send email.

What the person does #

When the person accepts the invitation, the gateway creates an account and a personal app called My first app for them, and shows the app's key once. They then follow Use a gateway and connect their apps with Apps & keys.

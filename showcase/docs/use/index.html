Use a gateway

On this page
  • Sign in
  • Choose what to do
  • What the owner can see

This section is for members: people who use a gateway that someone else runs. The person who runs it is the owner.

Sign in #

Open the gateway address that the owner gave you. If the page says Sign in to connect your apps, select Sign in.

Choose what to do #

Page Use it to
Try Send a message, make a picture, hear text spoken aloud, or turn a recording into text. See Try.
Library Find the pictures, videos and audio you made. See Library.
Apps & keys Connect an app you already use. See Apps & keys.
Usage Check this month's requests and open receipts. See Usage and receipts.

What you can use depends on the engines the owner connected and on your access plan. If a model or task is missing, ask the owner.

What the owner can see #

The owner can see your creations and your request activity. Your saved prompts are shown only to you in Library. Read Privacy and the owner for the details and the limits.

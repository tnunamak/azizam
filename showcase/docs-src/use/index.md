# Use a gateway

This section is for members: people who use a gateway that someone else runs. The person who runs it is the owner.

## Sign in

Open the gateway address that the owner gave you. If the page says **Sign in to connect your apps**, select **Sign in**.

## Choose what to do

| Page | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Try** | Send a message, make a picture, hear text spoken aloud, or turn a recording into text. See [Try](try.md). |
| **Library** | Find the pictures, videos and audio you made. See [Library](library.md). |
| **Apps & keys** | Connect an app you already use. See [Apps & keys](apps-and-keys.md). |
| **Usage** | Check this month's requests and open receipts. See [Usage and receipts](usage.md). |

What you can use depends on the engines the owner connected and on your access plan. If a model or task is missing, ask the owner.

## What the owner can see

The owner can see your creations and your request activity. Your saved prompts are shown only to you in Library. Read [Privacy and the owner](../concepts/privacy.md) for the details and the limits.

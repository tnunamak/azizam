# Owner visibility step 3: implementation plan

Status: proposed build slices, 2026-10-23. No product implementation or executed test evidence.

## Decision and evidence boundary

Make member work private to its maker on every product surface. An owner or administrator role adds no content authority. The owner gets a separate, typed operational view. A maker may share through the later sharing release. There is no owner-sees-all setting.

## 1. Policy to preserve while wiring the core

`gateway/content_access.py:247` is the target decision function for eight kinds and six actions. `:236` is its principal-intersection helper. `:301` supplies a uniform denied-content 404. `gateway/owner_projection.py:256` builds new typed values; `:334` projects the list envelope. These are different operations: ownership decides whether content can be read; projection decides which operational facts can be disclosed.

For creation, voice, job and receipt access, a session owns its personal and accountable service Apps (OV O6). That does not let the session read a personless prompt, chat or history row. Those rows belong only to the exact same non-null App credential scope. For person-bearing prompt/chat/history reads, require Self plus session, an attributed own output, or explicit `privacy_permission`. App-key own outputs use the same App with trusted App-key origin; MCP own outputs use the exact creating `connection_id`, never merely the browser session App. Missing legacy origin gives no own-output exemption. Debug read/delete needs Self and delegated permission; Debug reuse always denies.

Roles never substitute for these predicates. Receipt detail settings narrow an own receipt; they never enlarge ownership or the owner projection. Retention expiry, erasure and reader policy may only narrow the core. Resolve minimal trusted identity before authorization; do not serialize content, mint a link, poll, repair event rows or perform a mutation on a denied path.

In the maps, **O** means owner (including today's legacy `superadmin` where the code accepts it), **A** means administrator without owner, and **M** means another ordinary member. All three may read their own work. “Own” includes accountable service-App creations under O6; it does not mean “all work on the server.” “Bypass” includes a duplicated policy path that is private today, not only a demonstrated disclosure.

## 3. Read-path and bypass register

The step-1 frozen inventory has **464 route/method entries**, **93 classified as containing a content action**, seven tools and all `McpDeps` fields. These are source-fixture counts, not executed coverage. `tests/test_content_access_inventory.py:94` compares registration with the fixture; its module docstring explicitly says classification does not prove enforcement. Keep this as the inventory authority, extend it as routes change, and add behavior adapters. Do not replace it with another hand-maintained security framework.

The audit searched production root modules and `gateway/**/*.py` for the core callers, step-3 markers, receipt/job/history/Responses/prompt/voice store reads, raw SQL reads, metadata/byte readers and all signed-URL mint calls. ### Prior art and its limits

The existing corpus entry [Administrator content reach across 14 products](/home/tnunamak/code/divan/local-ai-gateway/administrator-content-reach-differs-across-14-products-by-role-api-and-elevation-so-azizam-should-default-to-member-private-prompts-and-creations.md), §§1, 5–6, 11 and 14, is the evidence behind OV's personal-library and operational-admin separation. Immich documents each account's own library and explicit album/partner sharing; Nextcloud separates account/quota administration from the account's Files and has explicit sharing. Those support keeping the owner an ordinary recipient and refusing to turn Everyone into consent. They do not prove universal admin denial on every API, and Immich partner sharing exposes metadata/onward sharing that Azizam v1 deliberately does not copy.

The same entry distinguishes optional impersonation, recovery and host/database access from a normal content view. It supports honest reset/host limits, not an assertion that a self-hosting owner cannot read storage. OV chose no v1 break-glass even though the research discusses one; do not reintroduce it by citing that broader research recommendation.

The existing [consumer AI prompt-retention survey](/home/tnunamak/code/divan/product-design/consumer-ai-products-keep-prompts-by-default-with-a-per-session-off-switch-while-family-products-show-organisers-metadata-not-content-and-embedded-prompts-leak-in-shared-files.md) supports a single per-request off switch, maker-private prompts and explicit per-item prompt inclusion. It also records metadata leaks and provider safety-retention limits, so Incognito copy cannot promise provider erasure. The [storage/quota survey](/home/tnunamak/code/divan/product-design/storage-quota-ux-separates-per-person-quota-from-server-disk-health-so-azizam-should-show-attributed-storage-in-usage-default-to-no-quota-and-pause-new-work-at-a-disk-reserve.md) supports keeping owner storage quantities separate from personal content, but belongs to OV step 4. These are previously captured documentary findings, not new web research or live product verification.

## Ledger conventions

A case ID is its full file path followed by :: and the exact test-title template, plus the explicit parameter tuple printed below. F01–F25 map to the full paths in the manifest. Line numbers are re-located at the pinned commit, not assumed from an older release. Inner assertion loops are preserved within their containing case; they are not additional test declarations. Prompt-memory member and household rows are separately expanded. Error objects and whitespace parameters have explicit descriptive values.

K = keep the original case and all its current assertions unchanged. K rows have the same old/new home and tier; no deletion survivor is needed, and no new mutant-proof claim is made. R+ = preserve original behavior while strengthening assertions or fixture synchronization; its detailed invariant/new-home/mutant/failure row follows the complete inventory. Additions listed as new-only do not inherit coverage credit from the original. U = pure unit, C = real component/QueryClient with external boundary fixtures, H = HTTP-client contract. Browser additions are a separate future tier.
